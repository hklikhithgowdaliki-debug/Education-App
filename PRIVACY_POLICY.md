# Privacy Policy – KSEAB Papers

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

KSEAB Papers ("the app") is a free study app that lets students browse and read previous-year and
model question papers for Karnataka SSLC, Karnataka 2nd PUC and VTU engineering.

## Information we collect
The app does **not** collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts,
no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no tracking.

## Network access
The app uses the internet only to:
- download the list of papers (`data/catalog.json`) from this GitHub repository, and
- download the question-paper PDFs you open, directly from their publishers' websites
  (for example vtu.ac.in, dpue-exam.karnataka.gov.in, kseab.karnataka.gov.in, karnatakaboard.com).

Those websites receive the standard information any web request contains (such as your IP address)
and are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Data stored on your device
Downloaded PDFs and the cached paper list are stored in the app's private storage on your device so
they work offline. You can delete saved papers from the **Saved** tab, or remove everything by
clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

## Children
The app is suitable for students of all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
