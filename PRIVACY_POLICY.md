# Privacy Policy – KSEAB Papers

**Effective date:** 17 October 2026
**Last updated:** 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the **KSEAB Papers** Android app (package
`com.hklikhith.kseabpapers`, "the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app is developed and
published by **H K Likhithgowda** ("the developer").

KSEAB Papers is a free study app that lets students browse, download and read previous-year and
model question papers for Karnataka SSLC, Karnataka 2nd PUC and VTU engineering. It is an
independent app and is **not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by** KSEAB, the Department of
Pre-University Education, VTU or the Government of Karnataka.

By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

## 1. Summary

- No account, sign-in or registration.
- We do **not** collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
- No analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
- Downloaded papers stay on your device and can be deleted at any time.

## 2. Information we collect

**We do not collect any personal information.** The developer does not operate any server that
receives data from the app. In particular, the app does not collect or access:

- your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details;
- your location (precise or approximate);
- contacts, photos, files outside the app, camera or microphone;
- device identifiers such as the advertising ID or IMEI;
- your search queries, browsing history in the app or the papers you open.

Searches and browsing happen entirely on your device and are never transmitted.

## 3. Permissions

The app requests a single Android permission:

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `INTERNET` | To download the list of papers and the question-paper PDFs you choose to open. |

The app does not request storage, location, camera, contacts, phone or notification permissions.

## 4. Network requests and third-party websites

The app connects to the internet only to:

1. **Download the paper list** (`data/catalog.json`) from GitHub
   (`raw.githubusercontent.com`), so new papers can be added without an app update.
2. **Download the question-paper PDFs you open**, directly from the websites that publish them,
   including:
   - `vtu.ac.in` (Visvesvaraya Technological University),
   - `dpue-exam.karnataka.gov.in` (Department of Pre-University Education, Karnataka),
   - `kseab.karnataka.gov.in` (Karnataka School Examination and Assessment Board),
   - `karnatakaboard.com`.
3. **Open links in your web browser** when you tap them (for example "Open in browser", this
   privacy policy or the issue tracker).

Like any website you visit, these services automatically receive standard technical information
needed to deliver content, such as your IP address, the time of the request and a basic user-agent
string. The developer does not receive, see or control this information. These services are governed
by their own privacy policies:

- GitHub: <https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement>
- The other websites listed above: see the privacy or terms pages on each website.

All downloads made by the app use encrypted HTTPS connections.

## 5. Data stored on your device

To work offline, the app stores the following in its **private app storage** on your device. Other
apps cannot access it and it is never uploaded anywhere:

| Data | Purpose | How to delete it |
|---|---|---|
| Downloaded question-paper PDFs | Read saved papers offline | Swipe a paper away in the **Saved** tab |
| List of saved papers | Show the **Saved** tab | Removed together with the paper |
| Cached copy of the paper list | Browse without internet | Clear the app's storage |

You can delete everything at once from Android **Settings → Apps → KSEAB Papers → Storage → Clear
storage**, or by uninstalling the app. Data is kept only until you delete it.

## 6. Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any personal data with anyone, because we do not collect any.
We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

## 7. Data safety (Google Play declaration)

For Google Play's Data safety section, the app declares:

- **Data collected:** none.
- **Data shared with third parties:** none.
- **Data encrypted in transit:** yes (HTTPS).
- **Data deletion:** all on-device data can be deleted by the user at any time, as described in
  section 5.

## 8. Security

The app downloads content only over HTTPS and checks that each downloaded file is a real PDF before
saving it. Files are kept in app-private storage protected by Android's application sandbox. No
method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but since no personal data is collected, no
personal data can be exposed by the app.

## 9. Children's privacy

The app is intended for students, including students under 18. Because the app does not collect any
personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from
children. It contains no ads, in-app purchases, chat or social features. If you believe a child has
provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help resolve it.

## 10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023,
or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. As the app
collects no personal data, there is nothing held by the developer to access, correct or delete. You
stay in full control of the data on your device and can remove it at any time (section 5).

## 11. Question-paper content

Question papers belong to their respective boards and universities. The app only links to and
displays papers published on the websites listed in section 4, and shows the source of each paper.
If you are a rights holder and want a paper removed, contact us and it will be removed from the
catalog.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version is always published at
<https://github.com/hklikhithgowdaliki-debug/Education-App/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md>, with the
"Last updated" date changed at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's
release notes on Google Play.
